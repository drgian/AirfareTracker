✈ FlightFare

Privacy policy

Last updated September 15, 2026

FlightFare tracks Delta Air Lines, American Airlines, United Airlines and Southwest Airlines fares for trips you choose and emails you when prices change. This page explains what we collect and why. FlightFare is an independent service and is not affiliated with any of these airlines.

What we collect

  • Your email address, to create your account, sign you in, and send price alerts.
  • Your password, if you set one. We store only a secure one-way hash, never the password itself.
  • If you sign in with Google: your email address and Google's account identifier, used only to sign you in. We don't receive your Google password, contacts, or anything else.
  • Your trips: airports, dates, optional flight numbers, cabin, and alert settings, plus the fares we find for them.
  • Anonymous page-view counts per day. These aren't tied to you or your device.

How we use it

Only to run FlightFare: checking fares for your trips, showing them to you, and emailing you about price changes. We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Services we rely on

  • Amazon Web Services hosts our servers and database.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.
  • Resend delivers our emails.
  • Google provides the optional "Continue with Google" sign-in.

Fares come from each airline's own public website.

Cookies and storage

We keep your sign-in in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your choices

You can remove any trip at any time with "Stop tracking." To delete your account and all of its data, email bigdaddyontheair@gmail.com from the address on your account, and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
